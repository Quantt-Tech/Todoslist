# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Todoslist a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on technical issues rather than people.
- Respect different experience levels and perspectives.
- Help create an inclusive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or deliberate disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sexualized or inappropriate behavior
- Conduct that creates a hostile or unsafe environment

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing community standards and may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the project's designated contact channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
